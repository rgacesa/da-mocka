# Privacy and disclosure

Synthetic does not mean anonymous. da mocka 0.0.1 has no real-data synthesis or privacy certification. Input checks do not authorize release. Future generation should avoid copying source records, regenerate identifiers, exclude raw free text and direct identifiers, suppress rare identifying strata and evaluate disclosure against a held-out reference. Exact-match and distance checks alone are insufficient. Consider membership and attribute inference, external linkage, and formal differential privacy where required.

Keep inputs, fitted models, source profiles, logs, detailed group counts and local audit reports in approved local storage. Do not commit them to GitHub. Review synthetic outputs and screened metadata under applicable institutional data governance before sharing. Neither a minimum stratum size nor a passed validation gate establishes anonymity.
